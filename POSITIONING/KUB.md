# KUB Radiography

## 1. Important exam idea
KUB means Kidneys, Ureters and Bladder. It is the basic abdominal radiograph.

Main points to remember:
- AP supine abdomen
- Also called KUB
- Routine abdomen view
- Used for urinary tract and bowel study
- Always mention patient position, CR, structures seen, and importance

---

## 2. Related radiological anatomy
KUB includes the region from:
- Diaphragm to pubic symphysis

Important structures seen:
- Liver
- Spleen
- Kidneys
- Ureters
- Bladder
- Psoas muscles
- Bowel gas pattern
- Spine and pelvis

Important pathology seen:
- Stones in kidney or ureter
- Intestinal obstruction
- Masses
- Free gas or fluid
- Foreign body

---

## 3. AP KUB view

### Indication
- Abdominal pain
- Urinary stone
- Bowel obstruction
- Mass
- Trauma
- Foreign body

### Patient position
- Patient is supine
- Arms are by the side
- Legs are extended
- Abdomen is centered to the cassette
- No rotation of the body

### Central ray
- Directed perpendicular to the cassette
- Centered to the iliac crest or midpoint of the abdomen

### Structures shown
- Kidneys
- Ureters
- Bladder
- Bowel gas pattern
- Psoas muscles
- Spine and pelvic bones

### Key points
- It is the routine abdominal radiograph
- It is very useful for urinary tract stones
- It helps in bowel obstruction and masses

### Simple diagram
```text
      X-ray tube
          |
          V
     Patient supine
     Abdomen centered
    Kidneys  Ureters  Bladder
       Bowel loops  Psoas muscles
            AP KUB view
```

### Exam answer
"AP KUB view is the routine radiographic projection used to evaluate the kidneys, ureters and bladder. The patient is placed supine with the abdomen centered to the cassette. The central ray is directed perpendicular to the film and centered to the level of the iliac crest. This view shows the kidneys, ureters, bladder, psoas muscles, bowel gas pattern and spinal/pelvic anatomy. It is useful in urinary tract stones, obstruction and abdominal masses."

## 5. Comparison table: KUB vs other abdomen views

## 9. Final revision summary
KUB is one of the most important abdomen views in radiographic positioning. It is the basic abdominal radiograph and usually the first view taken in abdominal evaluation. Always remember the patient position, CR direction, and the major structures shown. This is a very common exam question and easy to score if written in a correct order.
